**Focus: security.** This is the second review of a sensitive package. Review only for security and leave everything else to the first review: authentication and sessions, authorization on every entry point (who is refused, not only who is allowed), injection (SQL, shell, path, template, HTML), secrets and credentials, exposure of personal or confidential data (responses, logs, error messages, exports), unsafe use of files, commands or the network with outside input, irreversible external effects that can be repeated or replayed, and new dependencies. A missing refusal is a finding even when the happy path works.
